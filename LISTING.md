**Name**

Minecraft Skins

**Tagline**

3D body, bust and head renders, capes and nametags, straight onto your canvas.

**Description**

Put Minecraft skin renders on your Figma canvas. Type a username or drag in a
skin file, pick a view, hit import.

Six views: 3D body, 3D bust and 3D head, plus a pixel-perfect 2D face, a flat 2D
body and the raw texture.

Drag on the preview to spin the model, sideways for rotation and up and down for
tilt. Sliders are there too if you want an exact angle. Everything is rendered
inside the plugin, so the preview keeps up with the mouse.

Add a cape: the player's Mojang cape, their OptiFine cape, or one of the five
MineCon convention capes.

Turn on the nametag and you get a text layer above the image. Both land in one
frame so you can move them together, but they stay separate layers, so the text
stays editable and you pick the typeface yourself.

Your last ten imports are kept with thumbnails, and one click restores the whole
setup.

The panel follows your system language, English or French, with a manual
override.

The only thing that leaves the plugin is the username you type. Skin and cape
textures come from textures.minecraft.net, mc-heads.net, minotar.net and
capes.dev.
